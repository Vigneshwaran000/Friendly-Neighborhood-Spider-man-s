About This Website

This is a premium fan-made website celebrating the cinematic Spider-Man multiverse. It was built to be a one-stop interactive database for Spider-Man fans — bringing together characters, films, suits, villains, timelines, and trivia in one visually stunning experience.

Every section is designed to feel cinematic and immersive, drawing inspiration from Marvel's own promotional style while adding interactive features that go beyond a typical fan page — including a character quiz, a comparison system, a multiverse map, and even a mini web-shooter game.

All data, power ratings, quiz results, villain threat levels, and fan rankings are fan-created for entertainment purposes only. Nothing here represents official Marvel or Sony canon.

Cinematic History

The Spider-Man Legacy

From Sam Raimi's gothic New York to the multiverse-spanning Spider-Verse, Spider-Man's cinematic journey is unlike any other superhero's.

2002 – 2007
The Raimi Era

Sam Raimi's trilogy starring Tobey Maguire redefined superhero cinema. With organic web-shooters, gothic New York, and deeply emotional storytelling, this era proved that superhero films could carry genuine heart. Spider-Man 2 (2004) remains widely considered one of the greatest superhero films ever made.

OG Trilogy · Sony
2012 – 2014
The Amazing Era

Marc Webb's duology brought a skateboarder's swagger and a scientist's ingenuity to Peter Parker. Andrew Garfield's self-made mechanical web-shooters and chemistry with Emma Stone's Gwen Stacy gave us Marvel's most devastating on-screen romance. The death of Gwen Stacy in The Amazing Spider-Man 2 remains one of the most shocking moments in superhero cinema.

Amazing Series · Sony Reboot
2016 – Present
The MCU Era

Tom Holland's Peter Parker arrived via Captain America: Civil War and became the youngest, most technologically advanced Spider-Man on screen. Mentored by Tony Stark, equipped with AI systems and nano-tech suits, this Peter's emotional journey — culminating in No Way Home (2021) where all three Spider-Men unite — is the MCU's most celebrated arc.

MCU · Marvel Studios · Sony
2018 – Present
The Spider-Verse Era

Sony's animated Spider-Verse films changed everything. Into the Spider-Verse (2018) won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature and introduced Miles Morales to a global cinema audience with a revolutionary animation style that looked like a living comic book. Across the Spider-Verse (2023) raised the bar even further, becoming the most visually creative superhero film ever made.

Animated · Miles Morales · Sony

Theory & Canon

The Multiverse

The Marvel multiverse is the concept that infinite parallel universes exist simultaneously, each with its own version of events, heroes, and villains. Every Spider-Man comes from a different Earth — with different powers, different losses, and different legacies. No Way Home (2021) and the Spider-Verse films brought this concept to mainstream cinema in spectacular fashion.

EARTH-96283
Tobey Maguire · Raimi Trilogy

The original cinematic Spider-Man universe. Gothic, emotional, and groundbreaking. Organic web-shooters. The upside-down kiss. Uncle Ben's death and the burden of legacy.

EARTH-120703
Andrew Garfield · Amazing Series

A grittier reboot featuring self-built mechanical web-shooters and the most heartbreaking love story in superhero cinema. Defined by the tragedy of Gwen Stacy.

EARTH-199999
Tom Holland · MCU

The Marvel Cinematic Universe — Iron Man's world. Peter Parker is a teenager mentored by Tony Stark, armed with world-class technology, and forced to make impossible choices.

EARTH-1610
Miles Morales · Spider-Verse

Brooklyn's own Spider-Man. Miles gained unique powers — venom blast and invisibility — and learned that anyone can wear the mask. The heart of the animated multiverse.

EARTH-65
Gwen Stacy · Ghost-Spider

An Earth where Gwen Stacy was bitten by the radioactive spider instead of Peter Parker. She fights crime as Ghost-Spider while dimension-hopping across the multiverse.

EARTH-616
Peter Parker · Comics Origin

The primary Marvel Comics continuity. The birthplace of everything Spider-Man since Amazing Fantasy #15 (1962). Every cinematic universe traces its lineage here.

Built With

Technology Stack

This entire website was built with only HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no dependencies.

HTML5

Semantic markup, ARIA roles, and accessible structure throughout.

CSS3

Custom properties, glassmorphism, keyframe animations, grid, and responsive layouts.

JavaScript

Vanilla ES6+ — no jQuery, no frameworks. Data-driven rendering, modals, and all interactions.

Canvas API

Real-time animated particle web on the hero section using requestAnimationFrame.

localStorage

Fan votes, theme preference, and session state persisted across browser visits.

Web Audio API

Procedurally generated sound effects — clicks, thwips, battle sounds, and win fanfares.

Intersection Observer

Scroll-triggered reveal animations for all cards, timeline items, and section headers.

Responsive Design

Mobile-first CSS with breakpoints at 480px, 600px, 768px, 900px, and 1280px.
